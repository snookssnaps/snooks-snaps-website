THE WORK.

Behind Snooks Snaps

HI, I'M SAM.

I love photographing people, events and everything that makes me feel creative.

For me, photography is about more than capturing what something looked like. I want to capture what it felt like. The colour. The energy. The people. The little moments in between.

I love creating photos that are full of colour and life, and making people feel good in front of my camera. I want you to look back at my photos and remember the fun, the atmosphere and what it felt like to actually be there.
